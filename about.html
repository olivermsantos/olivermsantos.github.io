About Me

Bio

My name is Oliver Santos. I grew up in Forest Hill, Maryland, about an hour northeast of Baltimore. I graduated from Aberdeen High School in 2020, just as the COVID-19 pandemic was taking off. I spent my first year of college taking classes online from both UMBC and Harford Community College. In May 2021, I earned my Associate of Arts. As an undergraduate, I majored in mathematics, English, and political science. I also had minors in ancient studies and law and justice and was part of the Honors College. I am pretty sure one of my ancient studies professors was Athena in mortal guise, but she denies this. In 2024 I completed my undergraduate studies.

When I was junior at UMBC I did a two-week study abroad in Cymru (Wales) and fell in love with the country.
